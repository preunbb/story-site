# Structural review — reference

## Example findings

### Critical — contradictory injury state

> Ch.12 establishes both testicles ruptured; Ch.14 describes squeezing "his left nut" as intact.

Verdict: Critical unless an explicit healing/restoration beat exists between chapters.

### Major — ambiguous blocking

> "She kicked him from behind while he pinned her wrists above her head."

Verdict: Major — mutual pose is not stageable without an intervening reversal that is not narrated.

### Minor — pronoun drift

> "Lucas handed Andrea the clamp. She tightened it on his sack." (Andrea is "she"; "his" is clear from context but Lucas was last named three sentences ago.)

Verdict: Minor — re-anchor the antecedent.

### Ambiguous — repetition

> "The Goddess demands sacrifice. The Goddess demands sacrifice."

Verdict: Ambiguous — liturgical repetition vs. accidental duplicate sentence; note both readings.

### Not a hole — intentional misdirection

> Ch.18: Abby tells Elijah he won't get "a popped testicle today"; later neuters him fully on the dais.

Verdict: **Deception (confirmed)** — Abby uses "First Rite" in dialogue to mean one-ball breeding induction; victim was always a Second Rite (full castration) offering. Ch.14 lore + Eve's Ch.16 announcement support ground truth. Do **not** file as Critical continuity.

> Ch.18: Elijah recalls Abby promised it "wouldn't leave him a half-man."

Verdict: **Deception (confirmed)** — technically true if she full-castrates him (not half-man); Elijah parses as "no ball loss." Dramatic irony, not author error.

> Ch.17: Kay promises Isaac she'll take the broken right ball; pops the healthy left.

Verdict: **Deception (confirmed)** — Kay's established grooming pattern; Isaac's shock is the payoff.

### Hole — narrator contradicts ground truth

> Ch.12: narrator states both testicles ruptured; Ch.14: narrator describes intact left nut without healing beat.

Verdict: Critical — unless a restoration scene exists, this is not explainable as a character lie.

## Intentional misdirection — do not flag as plot holes

- Cult rite bait-and-switch (First vs Second Rite, breeding vs eunuch)
- Half-truths to keep victim calm until bindings are secure
- Kay performing piety while atheist (character hypocrisy, not lore break)
- Victim misremembering what they were promised
- Weaponized terminology in dialogue (same word, two meanings by design)
- Parallel chapter rewind (Ch.17/Ch.18 backstage) when timelines resync on a shared beat (scream, signal)

## Intentional repetition — do not flag

- Ritual call-and-response (Church rites, arena chants)
- Comic escalation (same move, worse outcome)
- Trauma/dissociation loops
- Parallel chapter structures (deliberate rhyme between scenes)
- Leitmotif words tied to a character or fetish beat

## Catalog-specific continuity hooks

When relevant, cross-check:

- **Same name = same person** across stories (`CLAUDE.md`, `data/characters.js`)
- **Castration/orchiectomy** — track per testicle; halfstration and permanent loss are plot-critical
- **Over Easy / Church** — capitalization and org rules per copy-edit preferences
- **Jenny** — unnamed dominatrix characters are Jenny

## Long-story strategy

For multi-chapter works (e.g. *Andrea and Lucas*):

1. Review one chapter at a time; keep a running ledger file in the response (not committed unless asked).
2. End each chapter section with **State at chapter end**.
3. Final rollup: only issues that survive chapter-level review + cross-chapter contradictions.

## When to stop

- User asked for a single chapter → do not expand to full-book unless asked.
- No synced markdown and Drive fetch fails → say what is missing; do not invent prose.
- Findings list exceeds ~30 items → group by theme and offer to deep-dive one area.
